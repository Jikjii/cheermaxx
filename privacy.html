CheerMaxx Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple identifier, and the email and first name Apple shares with us (you can hide your email).
  • Profile: the tumbling level, goal and practice days you choose.
  • Videos you upload and the scores, deductions and feedback generated for them.
  • Drill check-offs you record in the app.

How we use it

Only to score your passes, show your progress and build your training plan. Your videos are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the score and feedback; they are not used to train Google's models under our API terms. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase. Videos are private to your account and only accessible to you through the app.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Delete my data in the app to remove every video, score and drill log, or email us and we'll do it within 7 days.

Kids

CheerMaxx is built for athletes 13 and up. If you're under 13, please don't use the app. Parents can contact us to delete a child's data.
